PhotoCraft mask brush subset used by Upscale Lab

Upstream: https://github.com/storytold/photocraft
Pinned revision: 5f9b4391874aabc9deb2fcb905267613e4d93ec8
Sources:
- crates/algo/src/matting/brush.rs (segment distance, soft coverage, maximum stroke coverage)
- crates/algo/src/selection.rs (8-bit Add/Subtract mask combination)
- crates/algo/src/magnetic.rs (Sobel edges, ridge centres and corridor live-wire search)

Changes: translated the geometry and coverage subset to TypeScript; incremental input,
sparse canvas-sized tiles, source-coordinate replay, and application-specific limits.
Magnetic lasso uses a bounded preview field, piecewise corridor searches, typed arrays
and an approximate tangent term; its traced vectors are replayed at original resolution.
No upstream UI, model weights, fonts, icons, brand assets, or hosted API clients included.

not incorporated merely because this subset is used.
